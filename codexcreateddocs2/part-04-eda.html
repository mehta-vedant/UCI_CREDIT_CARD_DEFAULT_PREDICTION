What this means

EDA checks what exists before modelling: shape/types/nulls/duplicates, target distribution, distributions, outliers, correlations and group rates. Mean is arithmetic average, median middle sorted value, mode most common. Variance measures squared spread; standard deviation is its square root. Percentiles locate a value. Correlation is linear association—not causation.

How to look at a chart without guessing

A histogram groups numbers into ranges. A tall bar means many records landed in that range. It does not mean the value is good or bad. A box plot gives a quick view of the middle of a distribution: the line is the median, the box covers the middle half, and points beyond the whiskers are possible outliers. In credit data, a large bill is not automatically an error. It may be a valid high-balance client, so removing it only because it looks extreme would be careless.

A heatmap colours a table of correlations. Near-zero correlation means there is little straight-line relationship in the two columns. A high positive number means both tend to rise together; a negative number means one tends to fall as the other rises. The model can still use a feature with weak correlation, because tree models can learn thresholds and interactions. Correlation also cannot answer "what causes default?"

What the notebook did with EDA

The UCI notebook checks raw quality, draws target and feature distributions, groups outcome rates by demographic/payment fields, and experiments with derived columns. It is useful for learning what the data contains. It is separate from the current automated training run. The root training code does not load the notebook's engineered CSV as its source; it reads the raw CSV and builds a different set of features in Python.

How EDA should affect decisions

EDA should generate questions rather than premature conclusions. The strong association between recent delay status and default gives the model a plausible signal to test. The long tails in bills and payments tell us to pay attention to scaling and outliers. The class distribution tells us to report recall and precision. None of those observations proves that a feature should be used without checking held-out performance, fairness, and data-collection policy.

default.payment.next.month: 1 means default, 0 no default.

Beginner — Is this transaction fraud?

No. Root project is next-month client default prediction.

Beginner — What is a feature?

An input clue such as PAY_0 or LIMIT_BAL.

Beginner — Why test data?

To estimate performance on records not used to fit.

Beginner — What limitation matters?

It is historical Taiwan 2005 data, not production lending data.

Intermediate — Evidence for claim?

Cite active root source, paired artifacts, data inspection or recomputed metrics.

Intermediate — What ranks thresholds?

ROC-AUC; active GridSearchCV uses it.

Intermediate — Why can accuracy mislead?

Always predicting no default yields 77.88% accuracy and zero recalls.

Intermediate — What is leakage?

Evaluation/future information influences fitting.

Intermediate — What must accompany model.pkl?

preprocessor.pkl because it produces the expected 42 features.

Challenge — Best improvement?

Select on train-only stratified CV, reserve test, then choose threshold/calibration by cost.
