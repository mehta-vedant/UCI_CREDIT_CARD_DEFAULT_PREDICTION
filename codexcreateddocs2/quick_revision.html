“I built a supervised binary classifier for next-month credit-card payment default using UCI Taiwan data. The active pipeline creates 42 inputs, compares six models and serves CatBoost. On the saved 6,000-row split I verified ROC-AUC 0.784934, accuracy 0.820167, precision 0.664789, recall 0.359482 and F1 0.466634.”
